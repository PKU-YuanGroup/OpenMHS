## SDK: a sensed source with no producer stamp

A sensed-confirmation source (`safety_bindings["sensed_stop"]`, SAFE-049, CORE-088) may now set `freshness` to `arrival` when its producer does not stamp. The default remains `stamp`.

Arrival is ROS 2 only. The bridge requires `history` to be exactly `{"durability": "volatile"}` (no depth). The driver also requires that history to be cited (`ref` is `path:LINE`), with no `operator_required` and no `depth`. There is no `stamp_path`. `min_interval_ms` is a required integer from 1 to 1000. The first sample is discarded. A later sample closer than `min_interval_ms` to the previous arrival, including the discarded sample, restarts the count (`last_reset` `burst`). `min_span_ms` for that source is the arrival span of the counted samples. A missing, non-numeric, or non-finite field is still `sensed_invalid`. `confirm_within_ms` must cover `consecutive * min_interval_ms` for each arrival source. A profile may mix stamp sources and arrival sources. Transient-local history and ROS 1 are refused for arrival, because a replay spaced at least `min_interval_ms` apart cannot be told from live samples without a stamp. An understated volatile history is the same trust boundary as a stamp source's declared history.

G6's `frozen` filter still rewrites the producer stamp of a stamp source. On an arrival source it writes into the first predicate term's field, in declaration order. An equals boolean is replaced by its opposite, an equals string by a different string, an equals number by a different finite number of the same type (the next representable float, because `1e20 + 1.0` is still `1e20`; JSON `true` when no other finite float exists), and a numeric term by JSON `true`. A numeric field then fails the finite-number check and the attempt ends `sensed_invalid`. A NaN is not used: the recording transport copies with `allow_nan=False`.

Every post-discard arrival sample is validated before the burst rule. A missing, mistyped, or non-finite field ends the attempt even when the arrival interval is shorter than `min_interval_ms`.

`RecordingRosTransport.send` runs its handler without holding the inbound-queue lock. `buffered` takes that queue lock only. A handler that feeds terminal messages and waits for them can run while the receiver drains the queue.

Schema: `stamp_path` is no longer required on a sensed source; optional `freshness` (`stamp` or `arrival`) and `min_interval_ms` (1..1000) were added in `MHS/schema/device.schema.json`, `MHS/schema/reference.schema.json`, and the copies under `MHS/sdk/src/mhs/_data/schema/`. ## BL-18: spaced replay

No E2 runtime change. `RosBridge` fresh topic read-back still judges freshness by arrival, discards the first sample, and restarts the count when a sample is closer than `min_interval_ms`. A replay spaced at least that far apart is not detected. The rosbridge volatile-reader request is not proof. That limit is stated on the read-back docstring, in REVIEW.md item 13, in `sdk/docs/ros-driver.md`, and in SAFE-049 and CORE-088. The same limit applies to arrival freshness and is why arrival requires volatile history.

## Exclusion-ring overflow

The drain used to ignore `ring.overflowed` and confirm from the passive tail. It now latches `exclusion_fault` before that tail can set `exclusion_passive_at`, and a later passive sample does not clear the fault. The attempt ends `not_confirmed`. `ExclusionOverflowTests` in `test_sensed_stop_hardening.py` is the end-to-end regression: the observer reports `overflowed`, `safe_state_achieved` is false, and the holding gains stay `kp` 50 and `kd` 1.
