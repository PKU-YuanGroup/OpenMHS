## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: camera acquisition stopped (the native stop_grabbing); exposure and ROI stay configured and the camera stays open (achieved by `x-cap.pymodaq.stop_grabbing.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `roi_x0`, `roi_x1`, `roi_y0`, `roi_y1`, `binning_x`, `binning_y`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
