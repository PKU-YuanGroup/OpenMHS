## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `write`.
- Safe state: measurement aborted by the SCPI abort command (ABOR); a power read already running on the same VISA session is not cancelled and may re-arm a measurement (INIT) after it; the power meter is a passive detector and this package drives no output (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `power_range`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
