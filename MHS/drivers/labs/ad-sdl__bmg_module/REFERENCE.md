## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `set_temp`.
- Safe state: incubator heating off: the native set_temp(0.0) (Temp 0.0, the upstream's documented off value) acknowledged by an ExecuteAndWait response of 0; the temperatures are not driven down, and the plate tray and any assay started outside this package are not stopped (achieved by `x-cap.ad-sdl.bmg_heater_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `setpoint`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
