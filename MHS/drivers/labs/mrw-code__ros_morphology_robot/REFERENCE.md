## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: No acknowledged stop exists: the e-stop runs the upstream Exit path (exploration thread stopped, serial port and window closed) and reports MHS.SAFETY.SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held. Nothing is sent to the G-code controller, so moves it has already received may continue; nothing confirms that the stage stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
