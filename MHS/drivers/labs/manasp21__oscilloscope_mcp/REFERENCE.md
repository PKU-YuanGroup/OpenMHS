## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop_acquisition`.
- Safe state: acquisition stopped (the native stop_acquisition path) (achieved by `x-cap.manasp21.stop_acquisition.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `setup_trigger.holdoff`, `setup_trigger.level`, `acquire_waveform.timeout`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- EthernetOscilloscope is a stub: its constructor logs that Ethernet is not implemented and every method delegates to a SimulatedOscilloscope (upstream_code: src/oscilloscope_mcp/hardware/ethernet_backend.py:18-55)
