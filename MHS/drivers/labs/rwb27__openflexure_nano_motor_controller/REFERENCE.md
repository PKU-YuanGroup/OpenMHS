## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `release_motors`.
- Safe state: stepper motor coils de-energised by the firmware release command (achieved by `x-cap.rwb27.release_motors.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_relative.displacement`, `move_absolute.target_position`, `focus_relative.z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
