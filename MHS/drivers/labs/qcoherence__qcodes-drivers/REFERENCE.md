## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `go_to_zero`.
- Safe state: none proven: the e-stop sends go_to_zero (a 0 V command for all eight outputs written into FIFO 1, then both FIFOs cleared, with no acknowledgement or read-back; the process that executes it is not in the clone) and reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held; the real-time process keeps running (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `subsampling`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
