## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: acquisition stopped (ACQ:STATE STOP); the scope output is not software-controlled (achieved by `x-cap.trash-hold.stop_acquisition.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `vertical_scale`, `vertical_position`, `horizontal_scale`, `horizontal_position`, `trigger_level`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
