## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: acquisition STOP sent and TRMD? read back as stopped; no external circuit or WaveSource generator is stopped, and another client or the front panel can resume acquisition (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `vdiv`, `offset`, `tdiv`, `trigger_source`, `trigger_level`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- LeCroyScope is a VISA/SCPI interface for LeCroy oscilloscopes running MAUI firmware (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:198-200)
- Connection is lazy: __init__ stores the VISA resource string and connect() opens pyvisa.ResourceManager('@py') and the resource, sets a 10 s timeout and newline terminators, then queries *IDN? (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:209-237)
- The model is the second comma-separated field of the *IDN? reply (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:263-273)
- The model profile registry covers WaveSurfer, HDO, MDA, WavePro, WaveRunner and SDA families with 4 or 8 analog channels (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:53-156)
- get_capabilities returns the profile's channel count, bandwidth, ADC bits, coupling and bandwidth-limit values and math-channel count (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:275-294)
- get_channel_info queries <channel>:VDIV?, <channel>:OFST?, <channel>:CPL? (or COUP?), BWL?, <channel>:TRA? and, when the profile supports them, INVS? and UNIT? (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:440-460)
- set_vdiv writes <channel>:VDIV and set_offset writes <channel>:OFST (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:462-466)
- set_coupling and set_bwlimit validate the value against the detected profile before writing <channel>:CPL or <channel>:BWL (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:468-488)
- set_attenuation writes <channel>:ATTN (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:498-499)
- get_timebase_info queries TDIV?, TRDL? and MSIZ? (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:513-521)
- set_tdiv writes TDIV and set_trigger_delay writes TRDL (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:523-527)
- get_trigger_info queries TRIG_MODE?, TRIG_SELECT? and <channel>:TRIG_LEVEL? for every profile channel (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:536-548)
- set_trigger_mode validates against VALID_TRIG_MODES, set_trigger_source writes TRIG_SELECT EDGE,SR,...,SL,...,HT,OFF and set_trigger_level writes <channel>:TRIG_LEVEL (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:550-565)
- force_trigger writes FRTR, arm writes ARM and stop writes STOP (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:567-578)
- is_stopped queries TRMD? and reports True when the reply contains STOP (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:580-583)
- measure queries <channel>:PAVA? <parameter>; if the reply contains ',IV' it writes PACU 1,<parameter>,<channel> and queries again (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:628-640)
- calibrate queries *CAL? and returns the stripped reply (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:421-422)
- disconnect attempts to close the VISA instrument and resource manager, suppresses close errors, then clears the stored connections (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:239-251)
- get_date queries DATE? (upstream_code: oscilloscope.py:427-428)
- COMM_HEADER defaults to SHORT; OFF omits response headers and suppresses units (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:5063-5075)
- The manual states the attenuation factors {1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000, 10000} (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4527)
- The manual states the coupling values {A1M, D1M, D50, GND} (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4589)
- The manual states the bandwidth-limit values {OFF, 20MHZ, 200MHZ, 500MHZ, 1GHZ, 2GHZ, 3GHZ, 4GHZ, 6GHZ} (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4544)
- The manual states the trigger modes {AUTO, NORM, SINGLE, STOP} (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4842)
- The manual refers the timebase range to the product datasheet and gives no numeric TDIV bound (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4744)
- The manual says the offset range depends on the fixed sensitivity and refers to the product datasheet (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4660)
- The manual says an out-of-range trigger level is adjusted to the closest legal value and gives no numeric bound (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4818)
- The manual gives the trigger delay range as 0 to -10,000 times Time/div for negative delay and 0 to +10 times Time/div for positive delay (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4792)
- The manual says the trigger sources available depend on the oscilloscope model (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4884)
- The manual lists the trigger source mnemonics C1...Cn, LINE, EX, EX5, EX10 and ETM10 (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4914-4920)
- The manual says *CAL? returns a diagnostic code where 0 means calibration successful and the example reply for no failure is '*CAL 0' (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:6376-6381)
- The manual gives the PAVA response format <parameter>,<value>,<state> (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:5617)
- The manual says MEMORY_SIZE accepts a numeric size or the listed forms and the oscilloscope adapts to the closest valid memory size (vendor_doc: resources/maui_text.txt:4643-4646)
