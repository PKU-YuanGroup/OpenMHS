## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: pump stopped by STP; no infusion or withdrawal continues until a new run (achieved by `x-cap.rk-exxec.stop_pump.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `flowrate`, `targetvolume`, `pump.flow_rate`, `pump.volume`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
