## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `status-port selectRobot 1, halt, state`, latency 15000 ms.
- Safe state: TrajState 0 or 1 with power still on, and only when this endpoint has no unanswered MoveJ; RapidDecel does not retire a mailbox command. The arm may hold or sag, and this package does not re-enable power, attach or home and offers no unpause (achieved by `x-cap.ad-sdl.halt.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_joints.joints`, `move_joints.profile`, `move_joints.speed`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
