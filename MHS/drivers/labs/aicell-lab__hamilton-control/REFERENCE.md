## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop_shaking`.
- Safe state: No acknowledged stop: the e-stop sends Incubator.stop_shaking, which stops only the shaker, and then reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED; a plate transfer in progress, the door and temperature control are not stopped, since the front end has no stop or heater-off command and Machine.stop is teardown (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_temperature.target_temperature`, `start_shaking.frequency`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
