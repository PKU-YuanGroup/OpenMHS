## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `camera_object.StopGrabbing`.
- Safe state: camera_object.StopGrabbing, the call grab_image itself makes after a frame, is sent to end a running pylon grab; the capture's finally block unstages, which closes the camera, and outside a capture the upstream leaves the camera closed. An imaging sensor with no actuator: the stop proves no physical state; its effect while RetrieveResult waits or on a closed camera is pylon behaviour outside the clone (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `exposure_time`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
