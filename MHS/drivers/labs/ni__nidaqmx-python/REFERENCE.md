## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: measurement stopped (Task.stop): the task returns to its pre-start state and no longer acquires (achieved by `x-cap.ni.stop_task.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `sample_voltages.count`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
