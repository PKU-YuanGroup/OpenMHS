## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: all axes stopped; the native stop() is called on every axis (achieved by `cap.motion.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_absolute.positions`, `move_relative.displacements`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
