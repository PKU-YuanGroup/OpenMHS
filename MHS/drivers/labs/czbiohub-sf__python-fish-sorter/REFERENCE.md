## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: idle code (func_idle, 128 in the shipped config) written and taken by the controller (function register back at 0, echo register equal to the code); per the CoDeSys program text in the clone that step sets its Press and Vacuum outputs off (Press OFF, Atmos ON); not a sensed valve position, and the Zaber stage is not stopped (not covered) (achieved by `x-cap.czbiohub-sf.safe_pneumatics.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `draw_time_ms`, `expel_time_ms`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
