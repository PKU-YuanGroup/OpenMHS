## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: none confirmed: the e-stop queues the native stop 1I and reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED; the upstream reads no reply to 1I and has no run-state query, the stop waits behind requests queued before it, and once a request raises, the single worker thread ends and nothing queued is written, so the pump may still run (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `setpoint`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
