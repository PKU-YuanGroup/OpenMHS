## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop_acquisition`.
- Safe state: acquisition stopped (ACQUIRE:STATE STOP); the frozen display is left as it is (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `horizontal_scale`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
