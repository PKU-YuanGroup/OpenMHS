## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: pump stopped by the protocol T command of stop_operation and confirmed Idle by the Q status query; the valve and plunger hold their last positions, bytes already queued in the serial driver are not flushed, and a status reply other than the idle byte leaves the stop unconfirmed (SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED) (achieved by `x-cap.deepmodeling.stop_pump.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `position`, `max_velocity`, `pump.duration_s`, `pump.flow_rate`, `pump.volume`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
