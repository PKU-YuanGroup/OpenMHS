## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `emergency_stop`.
- Safe state: the tracked method is stopped with the native stop_method; without a tracked method the driver only queries the instrument status, because the client has no global stop request (achieved by `x-cap.aaronslaff.stop_active_method.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
