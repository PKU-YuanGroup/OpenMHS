## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: plunger motion stopped (the native stop sends T); the valve, the motor settings and the plunger position are left unchanged (achieved by `x-cap.croningp.stop_pump.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `transfer.increments`, `move_plunger.position`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
