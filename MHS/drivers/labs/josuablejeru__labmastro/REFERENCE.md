## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `write output=false; confirm output in [false]`.
- Safe state: Output disabled: register 0x0001 written 0 and read back 0. Setpoints and protection limits keep their values; the reading proves what the supply reports, not that a load is de-energized. (achieved by `x-cap.josuablejeru.output_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `voltage_setpoint`, `current_setpoint`, `over_voltage_protection`, `over_current_protection`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
