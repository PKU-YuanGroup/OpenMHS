## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `MOT_StopProfiled`.
- Safe state: stage motion stopped with the native APT profiled stop (MOT_StopProfiled) (achieved by `x-cap.mcleu.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `position`, `acceleration`, `maximum_velocity`, `move_relative.distance`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
