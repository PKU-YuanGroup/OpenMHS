## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: None reached: the e-stop closes this driver's SSH client, the upstream's only teardown (ot2controller_impl.py:159-161), and no line of the clone stops, pauses or aborts the remote python3 -m opentrons.execute process, so the protocol may keep running on the OT-2 after the e-stop and the e-stop is reported SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held (achieved by nothing declared).
