## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `output_off`.
- Safe state: output disabled (OUTPut OFF); the voltage and current set points are left unchanged (achieved by `x-cap.sergey12malyshev.output_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `voltage_setpoint`, `current_setpoint`, `voltage_limit`, `current_limit`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
