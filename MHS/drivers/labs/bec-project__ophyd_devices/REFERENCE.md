## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: No safe state is certified: PSISimplePositioner has no motor_stop Signal, so the native stop() writes the value read from user_readback into user_setpoint, a new target that the axis then moves to (a motor record may overshoot and return), and fails the running move status; the e-stop sends it and reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held, and nothing senses that the axis is quiescent (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_axis.target`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
