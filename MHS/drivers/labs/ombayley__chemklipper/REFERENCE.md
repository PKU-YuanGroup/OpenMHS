## Safety

- E-stop: both, latching, native stop `disable_motors`; physical stop: docs/user-guide/08-testing.md:18.
- Safe state: Gantry motors de-energised (M18); no axis is driven while the latch is engaged (achieved by `x-cap.ombayley.stop_motion.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_cartesian.x`, `move_cartesian.y`, `move_cartesian.z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
