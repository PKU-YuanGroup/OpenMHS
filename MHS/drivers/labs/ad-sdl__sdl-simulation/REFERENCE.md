## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world stays paused and get_physics_properties reads pause true: World::Step does not advance simulation time or call Update while the flag is set, unless a step is pending or a reset is due; this is the paused flag, not a measured standstill, and no unpause is offered, so recovery stays paused and the simulation stack is restarted before any manual resume, because the pending MoveIt goal, the navigation goal and the gripper publications cannot be shown to have been discarded. (achieved by nothing declared).
