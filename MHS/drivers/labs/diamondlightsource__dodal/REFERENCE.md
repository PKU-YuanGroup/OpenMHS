## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: Camera acquire cleared (disarm_detector runs after every e-stop); when the native stop() ran its cleanup with the operator's DetectorParams, Odin is also stopped, ROI mode disabled at full size and dev/shm streaming off (a stop() after an earlier stop or unstage only logs Already disarmed); a stop() or disarm that fails or exceeds the 1 s e-stop budget, or a stop() returning while an arming chain runs, reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED, latched and retried (achieved by `x-cap.diamondlightsource.eiger-disarm.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
