## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop_profiled`.
- Safe state: motor stopped with the native profiled stop (MOT_StopProfiled); the channel stays enabled unless disable is called (achieved by `x-cap.qpit.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `velocity_maximum`, `acceleration`, `move_to.position`, `move_by.distance`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
