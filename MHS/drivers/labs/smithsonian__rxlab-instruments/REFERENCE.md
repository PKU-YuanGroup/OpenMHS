### `scpi_rst`

`x-cap.smithsonian.scpi_rst.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R1, extension.

Call GenericInstrument.reset, which writes '*RST' (labinstruments/generic.py:65-68). The docstring says only 'Reset instrument' and does not state the effect on the RF output, the frequency or the power. An ordinary command: the safety reset does not call it, and it is not a re-enable or a safe-state action. A later read returns the instrument's reply and does not state what '*RST' set. The operator confirms the instrument, including whether the RF output is enabled.
