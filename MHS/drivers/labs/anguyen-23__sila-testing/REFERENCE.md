## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `calico.automation/extended/PhenixImager/v1/Command/Stop`.
- Safe state: No acquisition running, proven only by the controlled peer: after Stop, a fresh authoritative observation must show no active execution. A native Phenix gives no such proof: its Stop is an acknowledged RCI STOP, DeviceState is a cached status, and the stage is ejected only after a completed run (facts.md); the stage position and the plate are left where they were. (achieved by `x-cap.anguyen-23.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `phenix_connect.PhenixHost`, `phenix_stagein.PlateBarcode`, `phenix_stageout.PlateBarcode`, `phenix_gostandby.DurationSeconds`, `phenix_runacquisitionprotocol.PlateBarcode`, `phenix_runacquisitionprotocol.ProtocolName`, `phenix_runacquisitionprotocol.TimeoutMinutes`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
