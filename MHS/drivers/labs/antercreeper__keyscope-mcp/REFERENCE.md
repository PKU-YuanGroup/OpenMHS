## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: acquisition stop (:STOP) acknowledged by *OPC? = 1 and a zero error-FIFO code; this is command completion, not a sensed acquisition-state read; session close sends no :RUN (achieved by `x-cap.antercreeper.stop_acquisition.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `channel1_scale_v_per_div`, `channel1_offset_v`, `timebase_scale_s_per_div`, `timebase_position_s`, `trigger_level_v`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
