## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `close`.
- Safe state: RPC link closed through TEMClient.close; this client does not command the electron beam, the stage or the column valves (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `stage`, `beam_position`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
