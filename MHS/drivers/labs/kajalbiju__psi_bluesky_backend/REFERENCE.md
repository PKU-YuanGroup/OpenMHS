## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: temperature-loop setpoint written to 0 K (TSET 0), so the PID loop does not heat, and heater-loop setpoint written to 0 (HSET 0), the manual heater output (achieved by `x-cap.kajalbiju.heater_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `temperature_setpoint`, `heater_setpoint`, `p_value`, `i_value`, `d_value`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
