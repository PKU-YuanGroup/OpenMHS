## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `switch_off`.
- Safe state: remote control released (REMOTE -) and the venting valve closed (OUT_VENT 0); the clone has no pump-state query, so the pump state is not observable (achieved by `x-cap.tiagocoutinho.switch_off.v1`, `x-cap.tiagocoutinho.close_venting_valve.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `event_time_interval`, `on_setpoint_1`, `off_setpoint_1`, `on_setpoint_2`, `off_setpoint_2`, `on_setpoint_3`, `off_setpoint_3`, `on_setpoint_4`, `off_setpoint_4`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
