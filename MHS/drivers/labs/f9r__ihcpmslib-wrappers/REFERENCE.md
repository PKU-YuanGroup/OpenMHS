## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `Abort`.
- Safe state: temperature control off, CO2 auto boost off and normal CO2 flow off (SetTemperature(None, False), SetAutoBoostCo2(False), SetCo2NormalFlow(False)) (achieved by `x-cap.f9r.stop_incubation.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_temperature.target_temperature`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
