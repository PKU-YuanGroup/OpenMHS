## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Re-enable after a reset: `x-cap.kitour.run_acquisition.v1` (the reset never runs it; it is refused while the e-stop is latched).
- Safe state: acquisition reports STOP on :TRIGger:STATus? after :STOP and an empty SCPI error queue; this is a single acquisition-state readback, and this driver sets no instrument output (achieved by `x-cap.kitour.stop_acquisition.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `timebase_scale_s_div`, `timebase_offset_s`, `ch1_scale_v_div`, `ch1_offset_v`, `set_cursor.a_s`, `set_cursor.b_s`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
