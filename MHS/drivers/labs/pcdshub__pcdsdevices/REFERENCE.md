## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: trigger channel and trigger output disabled: disable_ch and disable_trg put 0 to the enable commands, and both read back 0 before the stop is reported; the readback is the IOC's enable records, not a measured absence of pulses, and equipment fired by other channels is not stopped (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `delay_ns`, `width_ns`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
