## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: heating and stirring off, with both setpoints reset to zero by the native stop methods; the serial line remains open until teardown (achieved by `x-cap.bamresearch.stop_hotplate.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `temperature_setpoint`, `stirring_speed_setpoint`, `heat_to.target_temperature`, `start_stirring.stirring_speed`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
