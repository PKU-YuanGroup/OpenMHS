## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `set_outmode`.
- Re-enable after a reset: `x-cap.jdmax.enable_heater.v1` (the reset never runs it; it is refused while the e-stop is latched).
- Safe state: the configured heater output is in output mode Off, confirmed by an OUTMODE? readback of mode 0; the stop command (OUTMODE <channel>,0,<channel>,0) also sets that output's control input to input <channel> and its powerup enable to 0; its heater range, setpoint and manual output are kept, the other heater output is not commanded and keeps heating if it was on, and nothing cools the sample (achieved by `x-cap.jdmax.heater_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `setpoint`, `manual_heater`, `set_temperature.target_temperature`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
