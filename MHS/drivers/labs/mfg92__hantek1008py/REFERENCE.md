## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `set_generator_on`.
- Safe state: waveform generator outputs disabled (generator off) (achieved by `x-cap.mfg92.generator_off.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
