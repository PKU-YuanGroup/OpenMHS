## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `continuous`.
- Safe state: continuous sweep off (INIT:CONTinuous OFF): the analyser takes no new sweep (achieved by `x-cap.davidlutton.stop_sweep.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `frequency_center`, `frequency_span`, `resolution_bandwidth`, `video_bandwidth`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
