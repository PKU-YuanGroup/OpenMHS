## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: no motion is commanded on the bound channel after the native Stop(0); the stage is left at the position where it stopped (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_abs.position`, `move_rel.offset`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
