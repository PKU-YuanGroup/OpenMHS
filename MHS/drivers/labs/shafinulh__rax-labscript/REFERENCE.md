## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `cam_stop`.
- Safe state: image acquisition aborted with camAbort (cam_stop) and the SDK acquisition query reports 0; the detector temperature target, the EM gain and the shutter setting are not changed by that call (achieved by `x-cap.shafinulh.stop_acquisition.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `exposure_time`, `em_gain`, `capture.exposure_ms`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
