## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: peltier target cleared, peltier output disabled, fan power 0 (M18) (achieved by `x-cap.opentrons.deactivate.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_target_temperature.target_temperature`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
