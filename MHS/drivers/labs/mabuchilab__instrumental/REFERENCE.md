## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `close`.
- Safe state: camera closed and its image memory released; no further frames are acquired (achieved by `x-cap.mabuchilab.close_camera.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `trigger_delay`, `exposure_time`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
