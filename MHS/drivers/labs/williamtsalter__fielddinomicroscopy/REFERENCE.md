## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `SetLEDState`.
- Safe state: LED off (SetLEDState(device_index, 0)); the microscope stays dark until an operator turns the LED on again (achieved by `x-cap.williamtsalter.switch_off_illumination.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `exposure_value`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
