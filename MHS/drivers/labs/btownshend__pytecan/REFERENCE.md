## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: unreachable through this client: Gemini defines no stop, abort or cancel method and close() is a pass, so a latched stop cannot stop a START_PIPETTING program or its plunger (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `variable`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
