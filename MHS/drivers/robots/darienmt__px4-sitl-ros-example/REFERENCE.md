## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/mavros/cmd/land`, latency 3500 ms.
- Sensed-confirmation stop (simulated devices only; SAFE-049): the request steps `/mavros/cmd/land` (service), `/mavros/cmd/arming` (service) prove nothing by themselves. The e-stop is achieved only when every source (`/mavros/state` (mavros_msgs/State, stamp `header.stamp`), `/mavros/altitude` (mavros_msgs/Altitude, stamp `header.stamp`)) delivers 3 fresh samples, on subscriptions opened after the request, that satisfy every term below within 1500 ms of the first transmission; otherwise it fails `MHS.SAFETY.SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED`, the latch is kept and the background retry sends the request again. A reset re-confirms without a request.
- Sensed state: landing requested; connected and disarmed on /mavros/state and the AMSL altitude still, sensed in three consecutive fresh samples within the bound.
- Predicate: `state.connected equals true`.
- Predicate: `state.armed equals false`.
- Predicate: `max - min of altitude.amsl over the window <= altitude_rest`.
- Threshold `altitude_rest` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest altitude bound; the operator supplies the largest AMSL change over the confirmation window that counts as landed and still).
- Client execution bounds (no source: a wrong value can only withhold the confirmation): consecutive 3, min_span_ms 200, max_gap_ms 500, confirm_within_ms 1500.
- Safe state: Simulated only: landing requested and the vehicle sensed landed and disarmed: /mavros/state connected and not armed and /mavros/altitude AMSL still within the operator's bound, in three consecutive fresh stamped samples within the confirmation bound. The land response alone proves nothing; a running offb_node may re-arm the vehicle afterwards; nothing is claimed beyond the confirmation window. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `setpoint_position.x`, `setpoint_position.y`, `setpoint_position.z`, `setpoint_velocity_body.vx`, `setpoint_velocity_body.vy`, `setpoint_velocity_body.vz`, `setpoint_velocity_body.yaw_rate`, `setpoint_velocity_ned.vx`, `setpoint_velocity_ned.vy`, `setpoint_velocity_ned.vz`, `setpoint_velocity_ned.yaw`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
