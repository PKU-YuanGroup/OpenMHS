## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: on the fake backend the software latch refuses step, reset and seed until mhs.safety.reset; the cached world state stays readable and releasing the latch does not advance it; no stop of the physical rotary-pendulum rig is offered (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- A reset draws the motor position uniformly from plus or minus 0.7 times MOTOR_SAFE_LIMIT_RAD and, for a hanging start, the pendulum angle uniformly from -0.05 to 0.05 rad (upstream_code: policy/pendulum_env.py:808-817)
