## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: after an e-stop no world step or reset is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, the fake does not advance and its last observation stays readable; the latch publishes no zero Twist on /mobile_base/commands/velocity and does not stop a kobuki, create or roomba base on that topic, and Gazebo stays paused only when each step or reset pause call succeeds, so after a failed pause or another unpause the last Twist keeps driving the base (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
