`x-cap.uthiras.effort_command.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/pause_physics`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: Two /get_model_list answers 250 ms apart share header.stamp in that confirmation window: a frozen sim time, not a measured standstill. A paused World::Step still updates when a step is pending or a reset is due (World.cc:835-841; WorldControl :1630-1639), and gazebo_ros2_control writes joint commands (gazebo_ros2_control_plugin.cpp:433-436). safety_status returns that cached confirmation. No unpause is offered: stop traj_follow and control_velocity, decide the effort, and unpause outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
