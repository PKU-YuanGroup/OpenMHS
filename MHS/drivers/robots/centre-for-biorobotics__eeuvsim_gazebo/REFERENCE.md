`x-cap.centre-for-biorobotics.fin_oscillation.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

Publish one Flippers message with the four fin oscillation commands on /hw/flippers_cmd. The receipt confirms only that rosbridge accepted the publication, not that moveFins applied it or that the AUV moved or stopped.

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/hw/flippers_cmd`, latency 2500 ms.
- Sensed-confirmation stop (simulated devices only; SAFE-049): the request steps `/hw/flippers_cmd` (topic) prove nothing by themselves. The e-stop is achieved only when every source (`/ucat/state` (gazebo_msgs/msg/EntityState, stamp `None`)) delivers 3 fresh samples, on subscriptions opened after the request, that satisfy every term below within 1500 ms of the first transmission; otherwise it fails `MHS.SAFETY.SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED`, the latch is kept and the background retry sends the request again. A reset re-confirms without a request.
- Sensed state: zero-amplitude flippers published; body linear and angular twist within the operator's bounds in three consecutive fresh samples.
- Predicate: `norm(state.twist.linear.x, state.twist.linear.y, state.twist.linear.z) <= body_linear`.
- Predicate: `norm(state.twist.angular.x, state.twist.angular.y, state.twist.angular.z) <= body_angular`.
- Threshold `body_linear` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest bound for the body twist; the operator supplies the largest magnitude that counts as stopped).
- Threshold `body_angular` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest bound for the body twist; the operator supplies the largest magnitude that counts as stopped).
- Publisher history of `/ucat/state` (a new subscription's replay, discarded before any sample counts): volatile, cited `eeuv_sim/scripts/AUVMotion.py:113`.
- Client execution bounds (no source: a wrong value can only withhold the confirmation): consecutive 3, min_span_ms 19, max_gap_ms 500, confirm_within_ms 1500.
- Safe state: Simulated only: zero-amplitude flippers are on /hw/flippers_cmd and the /ucat/state linear and angular twist are inside the operator bounds for three fresh samples. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_fin_oscillation.flippers[*].amplitude`, `set_fin_oscillation.flippers[*].frequency`, `set_fin_oscillation.flippers[*].phase_offset`, `set_fin_oscillation.flippers[*].zero_direction`.
