## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the fake simulated world stops advancing: the runtime refuses every step, reset and seed after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the fake's last observation and state stay readable; the latch calls nothing in AirSim, and this package offers no live backend (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
