## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/hydrodynamics/set_current_velocity`.
- Safe state: The fixed set_current_velocity request sets the velocity, horizontal-angle and vertical-angle model means to zero, and SetMean resets each process state to its mean, so the simulated flow is zero from the next world update while the velocity model's noiseAmp is zero (with noise it varies about zero within the configured range). It does not stop the vehicle or the Gazebo world. It is refused when any of the three configured ranges excludes zero; the velocity mean may then already be zero. (achieved by `x-cap.uuvsimulator.zero_current.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_current_velocity.horizontal_angle`, `set_current_velocity.velocity`, `set_current_velocity.vertical_angle`, `set_horizontal_angle.angle`, `set_vertical_angle.angle`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
