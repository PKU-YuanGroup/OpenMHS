## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No acknowledged ROS stop, pause or reset service or action exists in the pinned clone: /cmd_vel is an unacknowledged Twist publication, so this package declares no safe state and the stock e-stop only latches it in software without claiming that the simulated rover stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_cmd_vel.angular_velocity`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_velocity`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
