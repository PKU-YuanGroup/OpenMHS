## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the fake simulation stops advancing: no step, reset or seed is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the last observation, reward and tick stay readable; the latch calls nothing in HoloOcean and this package declares only the fake backend (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
