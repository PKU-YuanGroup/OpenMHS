## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the integration state stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- The repository is a collection of MuJoCo models of the Franka Panda ('Franka panda mujoco models') (vendor_doc: README.md:2)
- The README says the models adjust the default joint positions, with an offset of -pi/2 on joint6 and -pi/4 on joint7 (vendor_doc: README.md:10)
- The README says the joint4 range was reduced to avoid the gimbal-lock configuration (vendor_doc: README.md:11)
- The README says the joint limits were adjusted to avoid hitting the limits during operation with the hardware (vendor_doc: README.md:12)
