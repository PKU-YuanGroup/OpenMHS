## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The simulated fake stops advancing: after an e-stop no step, reset or seed is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the placeholder world state stays readable. This is a software latch on an isolated fake, not a robot stop; no hardware or ROS backend is served. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
