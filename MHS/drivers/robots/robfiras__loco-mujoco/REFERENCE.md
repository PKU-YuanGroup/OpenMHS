## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: after an e-stop no step or reset is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the last state stays readable and nothing is actuated (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
