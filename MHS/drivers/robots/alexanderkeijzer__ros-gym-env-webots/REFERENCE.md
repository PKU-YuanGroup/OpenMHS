## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The safe-state action is the fixed /webots/reset service: its reply acknowledges that the Webots supervisor accepted simulation_reset and that a later 8 ms time_step succeeded (at least two run). The Panda's ros controller runs with --synchronize, the configuration in which the world advances only on time_step calls; MHS sends none after the latch, but other ROS clients are not fenced. motorSetPoints is not cleared, and what Webots restores is outside the clone. (achieved by `x-cap.alexanderkeijzer.reset.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
