## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: after an e-stop the runtime latches and refuses step and reset until mhs.safety.reset clears it; the last observation stays readable and no native stop exists because the device is simulated (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- reset restores qpos and qvel from the initial state and returns the observation (upstream_code: myosuite/envs/myo/tasks/challenge/baoding.py:505-515)
