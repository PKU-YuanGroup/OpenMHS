## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the integration state is kept and stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- The upstream joint-space insertion environment loads bimanual_viperx_insertion.xml (upstream_code: sim_env.py:44-46)

- The insertion environment is built with control_timestep=DT and time_limit=20 (upstream_code: sim_env.py:48)
- The upstream joint-space action is 14 values: six left arm joints, one left gripper, six right arm joints and one right gripper (upstream_code: sim_env.py:23-26)
