- orientation_euler_callback publishes the roll, pitch and yaw vector with euler_pub and, when euler_based_orientation is true, writes the quaternion of roll, pitch and pi/2 minus yaw into pose_msg.pose.orientation. (upstream_code: src/duro.cpp:326-347)
- fake_pose_msg copies the header and position of pose_msg with a yaw-only orientation from fake_ori.getOri() + pi and is published by fake_pub; pose_pub publishes pose_msg when orientation_source is gps, fake_pose_msg when it is odom, and nothing for any other value.
