## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gnc/control`.
- Safe state: The fixed gnc/control STOP action commands the GNC controller's MODE_STOP and reports SUCCESS only after its EKF velocity and omega magnitudes fall below the configured stopping thresholds; this is an acknowledged native software stop, not a physical emergency stop. (achieved by `x-cap.nasa.astrobee.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move.states[*].pose.position.x`, `move.states[*].pose.position.y`, `move.states[*].pose.position.z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
