## Actions

### `publish_desired_state`

`x-cap.malintha.desired_state.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R0, extension.

Publish one geometry_msgs/Point goal to /mavswarm2/robot_3/desired_state. The receipt confirms transmission only; it does not prove that the SCP planner accepted the goal or that the simulated vehicle moved.

Inputs:

| Field | Type | Unit | Required | Limit | Description |

| `x` | number | m | yes | operator required (not set) | Goal x in the controller's NED frame (North) |
| `y` | number | m | yes | operator required (not set) | Goal y in the controller's NED frame (East) |
| `z` | number | m | yes | operator required (not set) | Goal z in the controller's NED frame (Down; negative is up) |
