## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: get_physics_properties read pause true (IsPaused). Ordinary World::Step updates stay suspended while that flag is true, except stepInc > 0 or a Gazebo reset (needsReset). Not a measured standstill. Other clients can unpause; MHS does not read the flag again. No unpause is bound. Before any manual unpause the operator prevents publisher respawn, including move_base, and clears or reinitializes stored x_ and rot_. Publishing zero is unacknowledged and does not prove they are clear. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_cmd_vel.angular_velocity`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_velocity`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
