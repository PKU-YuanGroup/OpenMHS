## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: An e-stop freezes the simulated world in software: no step is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the last observation and tick stay readable; this is a simulation latch, not a physical stop. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
