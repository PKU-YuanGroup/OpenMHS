## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the served model state, including the last controls, is kept and stays readable. A later explicit step without action reuses those controls (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
