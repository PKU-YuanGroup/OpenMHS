## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: Software latch only: the runtime refuses every step, reset and seed until mhs.safety.reset, and the last observation and tick stay readable. In a live MARA-v0 deployment Gazebo keeps running in its own process and the controller keeps executing the last published trajectory. The latch does not publish a zero JointTrajectory and does not stop a MARA controller on /mara_controller/command, including the real controller MARARealEnv uses. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
