## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No acknowledged ROS stop exists: the node offers only a zero cmd_vel publication, a 0.6 s no-command roll(0,0) timer and a shutdown-only stop() method, none of which is a ROS-accessible acknowledged safe state or an independent hardware e-stop. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_cmd_vel.linear_x`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_y`, `publish_color.blue`, `publish_color.green`, `publish_color.red`, `publish_heading.heading_rad`, `publish_angular_velocity.angular_velocity_rad`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
