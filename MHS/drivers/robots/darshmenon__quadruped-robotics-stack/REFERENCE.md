## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation is held: after an e-stop the runtime refuses every step and reset until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the model data stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
