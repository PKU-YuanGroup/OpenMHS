## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: the runtime latch refuses every step and reset after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears it, and the last state stays readable; the simulator has no native stop (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
