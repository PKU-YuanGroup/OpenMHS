## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/franka_spine_node/switch_off`.
- Re-enable after a reset: `x-cap.frankarobotics.spine_switch_on.v1` (the reset never runs it; it is refused while the e-stop is latched).
- Safe state: The spine drive is switched off through the acknowledged /franka_spine_node/switch_off service, which transitions it to SwitchedOff and disables motion; the separate halt service is not the safe state because it re-arms the drive to SwitchedOn. (achieved by `x-cap.frankarobotics.spine_switch_off.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_absolute.acceleration`, `move_absolute.deceleration`, `move_absolute.position`, `move_absolute.velocity`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
