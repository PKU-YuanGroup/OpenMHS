## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulated world stops advancing: after an e-stop every step and reset is refused and the last observation stays readable until the runtime's safety reset clears the latch (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
