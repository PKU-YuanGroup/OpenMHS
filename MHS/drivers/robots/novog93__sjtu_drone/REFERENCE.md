## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/pause_physics`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused: two get_model_list answers 250 ms apart carried the same sim time, so the drone plugin's update applies no force; it is a frozen sim time, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator lands or re-commands the drone and unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_cmd_vel.angular_x`, `publish_cmd_vel.angular_y`, `publish_cmd_vel.angular_z`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_x`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_y`, `publish_cmd_vel.linear_z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
