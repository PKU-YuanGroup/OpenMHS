## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `emergency_stop`.
- Safe state: No acknowledged stop: the e-stop sends zero motor-test thrust to motors 3 and 4 and a MAVLink disarm through the node, which only transmits them and reads no COMMAND_ACK or heartbeat, so it reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held; ArduPilot SITL and Gazebo keep running, a joystick or other MAVLink client can re-arm, and nothing shows that the boat stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
