## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true: the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: twist_command_ keeps the last cmd_vel (velocity_controller.cpp:169) and the behaviour node publishes again when simulation time resumes (behaviour.cpp:577, :592); an operator stops those nodes and clears the stored twist outside MHS, then unpauses. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_velocity.angular_z`, `set_velocity.linear_x`, `set_velocity.linear_y`, `set_velocity.linear_z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
