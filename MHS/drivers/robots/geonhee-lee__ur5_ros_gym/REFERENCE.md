## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: Software latch only: the runtime refuses every step, reset and seed until mhs.safety.reset and the fake stops advancing with its last observation readable; nothing upstream is called, so a live Gazebo world, which this package never drives and rl_world.launch starts unpaused, keeps running, and MoveIt still plans and executes an action already published on /action_move with go(wait=True), which nothing cancels (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
