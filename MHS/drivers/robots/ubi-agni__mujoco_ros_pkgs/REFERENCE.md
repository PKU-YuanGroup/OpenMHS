## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/mujoco_server/set_pause`.
- Safe state: Continuous simulation run is disabled (running=false). The paused branch can still execute pending or externally requested manual steps; this is not proof of quiescent physics, a frozen clock or a physical emergency stop. (achieved by `x-cap.ubi-agni.pause.v1`).
