- Safe state: the declared analytic fake stops advancing and keeps its state and cached targets readable; no step or world reset is accepted while latched. mhs.safety.reset releases only the latch and advances no ticks. No pause or stop of an external Isaac Sim application or serial SO101Leader is provided; the serial device is outside this package (achieved by nothing declared).
