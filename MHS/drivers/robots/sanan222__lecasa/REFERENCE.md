## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: an e-stop latches the runtime and no further step or reset reaches the model until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; time, qpos, qvel and ctrl remain readable and unchanged (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
