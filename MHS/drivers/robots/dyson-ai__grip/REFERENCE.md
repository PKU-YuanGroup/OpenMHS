## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: new environment calls are fenced immediately; idle is confirmed only after any in-flight native call returns; reset refuses while a call is active; the last state stays readable (achieved by `x-cap.dyson-ai.fence.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- reset restores the world state, resets the panda and returns obs() (upstream_code: grip/environments/template_env.py:148-155)

- PandaGripper.apply_gripper_delta_action takes a delta finger-distance action, adds it to the finger positions and clips each finger to its lower and upper joint limit (upstream_code: grip/robot/panda_gripper.py:54-65)
