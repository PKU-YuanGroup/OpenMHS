## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/all/emergency`, latency 2500 ms.
- Terminal e-stop: the e-stop asks each Crazyflie's firmware for an emergency stop that nothing in the clone undoes; only a restart of the simulated firmware and server, a new native session, recovers it. Every reset after an e-stop on the same endpoint is refused (`terminal_estop`).
- Sensed-confirmation stop (simulated devices only; SAFE-049): the request steps `/all/emergency` (service) prove nothing by themselves. The e-stop is achieved only when every source (`/cf_1/pose` (geometry_msgs/msg/PoseStamped, stamp `header.stamp`)) delivers 3 fresh samples, on subscriptions opened after the request, that satisfy every term below within 1500 ms of the first transmission; otherwise it fails `MHS.SAFETY.SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED`, the latch is kept and the background retry sends the request again. A reset re-confirms without a request.
- Sensed state: rotors cut by the firmware emergency stop of every enabled simulated Crazyflie; cf_1 sensed on the ground and still on /cf_1/pose in three consecutive fresh samples within the bound.
- Predicate: `pose.pose.position.z <= ground_z.max`.
- Predicate: `max - min of pose.pose.position.x over the window <= rest_x`.
- Predicate: `max - min of pose.pose.position.y over the window <= rest_y`.
- Predicate: `max - min of pose.pose.position.z over the window <= rest_z`.
- Threshold `ground_z.max` (max): operator_required (the clone states no on-ground band; the configured initial_position z 0.0 (config/crazyflies_mrs.yaml:17) is a reference, not a bound, so the operator supplies it).
- Threshold `rest_x` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest position bound; the operator supplies the largest position change over the confirmation window that counts as at rest).
- Threshold `rest_y` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest position bound; the operator supplies the largest position change over the confirmation window that counts as at rest).
- Threshold `rest_z` (max): operator_required (the clone states no at-rest position bound; the operator supplies the largest position change over the confirmation window that counts as at rest).
- Publisher history of `/cf_1/pose` (a new subscription's replay, discarded before any sample counts): volatile, cited `to_copy/crazyflie_server.py:404-405`.
- Client execution bounds (no source: a wrong value can only withhold the confirmation): consecutive 3, min_span_ms 200, max_gap_ms 500, confirm_within_ms 1500.
- Safe state: Simulated only: rotors cut by the firmware emergency stop of every enabled simulated Crazyflie, and cf_1 sensed on the ground and still on /cf_1/pose in three consecutive fresh stamped samples within the confirmation bound. The Empty response alone proves nothing; nothing is claimed beyond the confirmation window, and the stop is terminal on this endpoint. (achieved by nothing declared).
