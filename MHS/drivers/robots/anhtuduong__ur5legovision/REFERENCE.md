## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: Gazebo physics paused by the acknowledged /gazebo/pause_physics service: the simulated arm and its controller freeze, and a running /ur5/move_joints or /ur5/move_gripper callback is held in its simulated-time rate.sleep(), not cancelled; nothing senses quiescence. mhs.safety.reset clears only the latch and leaves Gazebo paused; the explicit resume command unpauses it, and an interrupted motion then continues. With real_robot set there is no Gazebo and this stop fails. (achieved by `x-cap.anhtuduong.pause.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_gripper.diameter_mm`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
