## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the MuJoCo integration state is kept and stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `step.action.ctrl`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
