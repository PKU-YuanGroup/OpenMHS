## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `step_simulation`.
- Safe state: No acknowledged safe state: the e-stop sends the constructor's ControlWorld pause request through step_simulation, which returns None whether or not the call was answered and never reads response.success, then reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held; a pause that reaches Gazebo leaves the world paused until something resumes it, unconfirmed, and external ROS controllers and cflib clients are not stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
