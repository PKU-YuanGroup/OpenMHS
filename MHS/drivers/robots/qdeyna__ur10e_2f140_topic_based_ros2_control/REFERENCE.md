## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/controller_manager/switch_controller`.
- Re-enable after a reset: `x-cap.qdeyna.activate_controller.v1` (the reset never runs it; it is refused while the e-stop is latched).
- Safe state: scaled_joint_trajectory_controller is inactive, as list_controllers read back: its goal was aborted and its trajectory dropped, and each joint's position command keeps its last value; the world keeps running, it is the controller's state and not a measured standstill, other arm controllers and the gripper are not shown inactive, and any client can re-activate it. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `follow_arm_trajectory.points[*].positions`, `follow_arm_trajectory.points[*].time_from_start.nanosec`, `follow_arm_trajectory.points[*].time_from_start.sec`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
