## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/franka_gripper/stop`.
- Safe state: The fixed StopAction acknowledges the native gripper.stop() result. Older pending goals must settle before a fresh stop can confirm safety. This is a software native stop, not a physical e-stop; stopping grasp force may release a load. (achieved by `x-cap.frankarobotics.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `grasp.force`, `grasp.width`, `move_gripper.speed`, `move_gripper.width`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
