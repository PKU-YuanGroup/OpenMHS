## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulator stops advancing: after an e-stop no reset or step is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, while the last observation stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- Action 1 applies a push of +force_mag and any other valid action applies -force_mag (upstream_code: robust_gymnasium/envs/classic_control/cartpole.py:169)
