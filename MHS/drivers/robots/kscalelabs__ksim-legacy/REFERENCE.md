## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: bridge-owned lockstep integration stops; no step or world reset is accepted while latched. The integration state, velocities and controls are retained and readable. mhs.safety.reset only clears the latch; a new step is required and a step without action reuses retained controls (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
