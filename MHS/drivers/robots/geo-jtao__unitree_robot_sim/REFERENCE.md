## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The simulation stops advancing: after an e-stop no cap.world.step.v1 or cap.world.reset.v1 call is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, while the integration state stays readable. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
