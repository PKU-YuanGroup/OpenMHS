## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the environment state is kept and stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- SO101LiftCube-v1 is registered through mani_skill.utils.registration.register_env with max_episode_steps=50, and LiftCube fixes item_type to cube (upstream_code: envs/lift.py:407-410)
- The Lift environment defaults to robot_uids='so101' and control_mode='pd_joint_target_delta_pos' (upstream_code: envs/lift.py:59-61)
- The pd_joint_target_delta_pos controller is the six-joint delta controller (use_delta=True): its lower bounds are -0.1 for the five arm joints and -0.2 for the gripper, and its upper bounds are 0.1 and 0.2; it does not set normalize_action, and the target variant only sets use_target=True (upstream_code: envs/robot/so101.py:97-109)
- The default simulation config is SimConfig(sim_freq=100, control_freq=10): one control step advances 0.1 s of simulated time (upstream_code: envs/base_random_env.py:146-148)
- The wrist camera renders 128x128 images (upstream_code: envs/base_random_env.py:521-527)
- The trainer renders at render_size 128 and downsamples the policy input to image_size 16 (upstream_code: train_squint.py:107-110)
- The state observation includes qvel, is_item_grasped, item_pose, tcp_pos, the item-to-tcp vector and the joint target distance to the rest pose (upstream_code: envs/lift.py:328-341)
- Success requires the item to be lifted, grasped and the arm to be within 0.2 of the rest joint pose (upstream_code: envs/lift.py:354-373)
