## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the software latch refuses further step, reset and seed calls until mhs.safety.reset; cached world state stays readable and latch reset advances nothing; the latch does not interrupt an in-flight SAPIEN planner or certify a native stop; no hardware path is served (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
