## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the environment state is kept and stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- TDW-MAT extends the ThreeDWorld Transport Challenge into a multi-agent setting on top of the TDW platform (vendor_doc: tdw_mat/README.md:77)
- The environment class is TDW, a subclass of the legacy Gym Env (upstream_code: tdw_mat/tdw-gym/tdw_gym.py:50)
- gym id transport_challenge_MA is registered with entry point tdw_gym:TDW (upstream_code: tdw_mat/tdw-gym/__init__.py:4-7)
- The single-agent action space is a Dict of type Discrete(7), object Discrete(30), arm Discrete(2) and a message Text of at most 1000 characters (upstream_code: tdw_mat/tdw-gym/tdw_gym.py:104-108)
- The action type 0 moves forward 0.5 m, 1 turns left 15 degrees, 2 turns right 15 degrees, 3 reaches for and grasps, 4 puts in a container, 5 drops and 6 sends a message; every other type asserts False (upstream_code: tdw_mat/tdw-gym/tdw_gym.py:628-644)
