## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and the read-back reported pause true. World::Step still updates when a step is pending or a reset is due; otherwise gazebo_ros_control writes no effort. The flag is not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered. Stopping panda_control_AIC does not clear retained joint effort or the AIC torque state u. The world stays paused until an operator-reviewed procedure has independently verified that those retained controller commands are cleared or reinitialized. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_joint1_effort.effort`, `set_joint2_effort.effort`, `set_joint3_effort.effort`, `set_joint4_effort.effort`, `set_joint5_effort.effort`, `set_joint6_effort.effort`, `set_joint7_effort.effort`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
