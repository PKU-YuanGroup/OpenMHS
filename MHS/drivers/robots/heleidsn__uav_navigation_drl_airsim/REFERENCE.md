## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: while the e-stop is latched, step and reset calls are refused with MHS.SAFETY.ESTOP_ENGAGED and the last state stays readable; only mhs.safety.reset clears the latch (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
