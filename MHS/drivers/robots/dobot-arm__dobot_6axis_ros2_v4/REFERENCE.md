## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Re-enable after a reset: `x-cap.dobot-arm.enable.v1` (the reset never runs it; it is refused while the e-stop is latched).
- Safe state: Unconfirmed: no native e-stop is bound because the upstream Stop response can retain res=0 after a failed TCP exchange. The latch blocks new MHS commands but neither sends a dashboard Stop nor establishes a stopped arm; motion already running may continue. The ordinary Stop service and an explicit latch reset confirm no hardware state. A reviewed stop/confirmation path and independent physical stop are required outside this blocked package. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_joint.joint_positions`, `move_pose.orientation.pitch`, `move_pose.orientation.roll`, `move_pose.orientation.yaw`, `move_pose.position.x`, `move_pose.position.y`, `move_pose.position.z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
