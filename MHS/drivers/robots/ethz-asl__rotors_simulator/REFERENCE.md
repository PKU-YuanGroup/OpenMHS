## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true back: no world update runs, so the motor model applies no rotor force and odometry and IMU are silent; it is the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator restarts or re-commands the controller and unpauses outside MHS (README.md:145-150). (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
