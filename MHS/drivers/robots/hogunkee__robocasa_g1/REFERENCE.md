## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: the e-stop closes the environment and no step or reset is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch and reopens it at the recorded seed; the observation and tick stay readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
