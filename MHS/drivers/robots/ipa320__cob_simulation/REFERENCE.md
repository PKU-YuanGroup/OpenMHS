## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true back; the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: controllers this package does not command keep their last command (robot.launch:12, ipa-apartment.xml:13-14). The Gazebo GUI or another client can unpause or step the world (a step advances it). World edits still act while paused. After mhs.safety.reset the world stays paused; the operator stops those nodes, then unpauses outside MHS (world.launch:14). (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
