## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `stop`.
- Safe state: every motor's torque limit is 0 and the EtherCAT motor loop has ended (it left operational state, requested INIT and closed the socket); the biped has no joint torque and falls unless it is supported; the sensor collector and the UDP command receiver keep running (achieved by `x-cap.generalroboticslab.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `command_forward`, `command_lateral`, `command_yaw`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
