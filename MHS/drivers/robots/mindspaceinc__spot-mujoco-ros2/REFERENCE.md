## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/simulation/sim_reset`.
- Safe state: Simulated reset, acknowledged after the writes: /simulation/sim_reset calls mj_resetData, puts the base at (0, 0, 0.7) with the model-default hinge positions and zero velocities, and zeroes every cached actuator command, so apply_ctrl writes zero control (joint damping kd 1.0 once the 0.2 s stale timer fires). Physics keeps running unless paused in the GUI: the limp robot falls and settles on the floor. A later actuators_cmds message from any client re-commands it. Not a physical emergency stop. (achieved by `x-cap.mindspaceinc.safe_reset.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
