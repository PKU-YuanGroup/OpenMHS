## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: Software latch only: the runtime refuses every step and reset until mhs.safety.reset, the last observation and tick stay readable, and the e-stop sends nothing to Gazebo. In a live CartpoleEnv-v0 deployment each completed step or reset ends with a WorldControl pause request whose reply is not inspected, so the world stays paused only if that request took effect; a step or reset in flight keeps the world unpaused, waiting without a timeout, until its next /joint_states sample arrives. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
