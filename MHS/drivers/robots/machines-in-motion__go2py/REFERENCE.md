## Safety

- E-stop: both, latching, native stop `setCommandsLow`; physical stop: deploy/ros2_nodes/go2py_bridge/src/bridge.cpp:234-243.
- Safe state: zero body velocity (the bridge sends Move(0, 0, 0)) and the bridge's low-level damping command, kp 0 and kd 3 with zero torque, to all 12 motors; the wireless remote's R2 and L2 make the bridge add its sport-mode Damp request and latch Estop until an operator clears it with L2 and A (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `drive.forward_velocity`, `drive.lateral_velocity`, `drive.yaw_rate`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- GO2Real is the clone's user-facing robot interface class (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:24)
- GO2Real creates a CycloneDDS participant, a state DataReader and low- and high-command DataWriters (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:59-70)
- getJointStates returns the joint angles q, velocities dq, estimated torques tau_est and motor temperatures (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:106-111)
- getIMU returns acceleration, angular velocity, quaternion and IMU temperature (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:94-99)
- getFootContacts returns the raw foot contact forces (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:101-104)
- getBatteryState returns the battery percentage (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:159-161)
- setCommandsHigh writes v_x, v_y and omega_z to the high-level command writer (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:163-171)
- setCommandsLow requires q_des, dq_des, kp, kd and tau_ff arrays of 12 elements and writes them to the low-level command writer (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/interface.py:173-190)
- The bridge names the 12 motor_state entries FR, FL, RR, RL and copies them in that order into go2py_state q, dq, tau and motor_temp (upstream_code: deploy/ros2_nodes/go2py_bridge/src/bridge.cpp:188-219)
- The bridge sets Estop = 1 when the wireless remote's R2 and L2 buttons are pressed and Estop = 0 with L2 and A (upstream_code: deploy/ros2_nodes/go2py_bridge/src/bridge.cpp:234-243)
- While Estop is set the bridge sends a damp request and a zero low-level command with kd 3 to all 12 motors (upstream_code: deploy/ros2_nodes/go2py_bridge/src/bridge.cpp:317-333)
- The upstream FSM's tracking move uses kp 150 and kv 3 and its damping mode uses kd 2 (upstream_code: Go2Py/robot/fsm.py:15-18)
- The URDF states the position range, effort and velocity of every leg joint (upstream_code: Go2Py/assets/urdf/go2.urdf:99-597)
- The MuJoCo model states the motor torque control range as +-23.7 for the hip and thigh motors and +-45.43 for the calf motors (upstream_code: Go2Py/assets/mujoco/go2.xml:10-24)
- The README describes the C++ bridge as implementing a remote-controlled emergency stop (vendor_doc: README.md:10)
