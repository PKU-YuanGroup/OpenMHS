## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/world/sydney_regatta/control`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: The world sydney_regatta is paused: after the WorldControl pause two fresh stats samples, 40 ms or more apart within 600 ms, reported paused true, so the world does not step and the thrusters apply no thrust; it is the paused flag in that window, not a measured standstill, it needs the operator-run ros_gz bridges, and no unpause is offered: stored thrust and angle targets, waves and wind act when the operator resumes it. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_left_thrust.thrust`, `publish_right_thrust.thrust`, `publish_left_thrust_position.position`, `publish_right_thrust_position.position`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
