### `read_imu`

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The IMU is a passive sensor: it has no actuation, motion, stop or reset command, so the driver only reads observations and commands no safe state. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

- The hfi-a9 parser reads a 49-byte 0xAA 0x55 0x2c frame of nine little-endian floats, stores wx, wy and wz as IMU_WX, IMU_WY and IMU_WZ, stores -9.8 times ax, ay and az as IMU_X_ACC, IMU_Y_ACC and IMU_Z_ACC and the three magnetometer values, then reads a 25-byte 0xAA 0x55 0x14 frame of roll, pitch and yaw in degrees, converts them to radians and stores the resulting quaternion in IMU_ORI_QUAT_X, IMU_ORI_QUAT_Y, IMU_ORI_QUAT_Z and IMU_ORI_QUAT_W before it appends one observation. (upstream_code: modules/mrpt_hwdrivers/src/CTaoboticsIMU.cpp:248-389)
- The hfi-b6 parser reads 11-byte frames; it computes the acceleration only to print it, stores nothing for the angular-velocity frame, and creates an observation only on the euler frame, without setting a measurement. (upstream_code: modules/mrpt_hwdrivers/src/CTaoboticsIMU.cpp:144-246)
