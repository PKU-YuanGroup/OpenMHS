## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: Software latch over a synchronous simulator: the dm_control composer world integrates physics only inside a native step or reset, so after an e-stop no step or reset is dispatched until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and a native step already running completes (the e-stop waits for it within its budget) before the e-stop confirms. There is nothing physical: two simulated Shadow Hands and a simulated piano. (achieved by nothing declared).
