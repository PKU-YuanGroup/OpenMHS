## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the runtime latches the e-stop and accepts no step or reset until mhs.safety.reset clears it; the fake holds its last observation and tick, and the latch does not disarm the vehicle or clear the PX4Vehicle.set_pose2d setpoint, so a physical PX4 on that MAVROS link is not stopped. A completed step or reset pauses Gazebo only when /gazebo/pause_physics succeeds; a failed pause is only printed, and a running step or reset is not interrupted (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
