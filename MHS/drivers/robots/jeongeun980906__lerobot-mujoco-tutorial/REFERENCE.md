## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the bridge-owned simulation stops advancing: no step or world reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, while the integration state and stored ctrl stay readable; releasing the latch does not integrate or clear ctrl, and a new explicit step without an action reuses the stored position targets (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
