## Actions

### `publish_cmd_vel`

`x-cap.agilexrobotics.cmd_vel.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

Publish geometry_msgs/Twist linear.x and angular.z on /cmd_vel. TwistCmdCallback converts them to four Float64 wheel commands and stores no Twist. The receipt is transmission evidence and the values sent, not a measured velocity and not a cleared controller.
