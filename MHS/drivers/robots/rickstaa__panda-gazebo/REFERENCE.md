## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/lock_unlock_panda_joints`.
- Safe state: Every Panda arm and finger joint has its Gazebo lower and upper limits set to the position it had when the /lock_unlock_panda_joints callback ran. The service acknowledges that limit update, not a measured zero velocity; Gazebo keeps running, and the trajectory controller, MoveIt or any other ROS client may keep commanding the locked joints. The lock persists after mhs.safety.reset: no unlock is declared, and repeated lock requests overwrite the limits the plugin stores for an unlock. (achieved by `x-cap.rickstaa.lock_joints.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `follow_joint_trajectory.points[*].positions`, `follow_joint_trajectory.time_axis_step`, `follow_joint_trajectory.timeout_s`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
