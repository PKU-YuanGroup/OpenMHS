## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: Software latch only: after an e-stop the bridge refuses step and reset until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the fake's state is kept and stays readable. Nothing is sent to the environment: a live Gazebo is free-running and would keep simulating with the last /cmd_vel Twist in place, because the upstream publishes velocity only from step and calls no pause service. The latch does not publish a zero Twist, so a physical base subscribed to /cmd_vel is not stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
