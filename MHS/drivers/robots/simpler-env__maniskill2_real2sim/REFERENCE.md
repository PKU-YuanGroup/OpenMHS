## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No new step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch. A native step or reset already running is not interrupted: it completes (a multi-step command stops at the next control call), and the e-stop reports the safe state only after it ended, waiting at most the e-stop budget and otherwise retrying in the background. The world state stays readable. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
