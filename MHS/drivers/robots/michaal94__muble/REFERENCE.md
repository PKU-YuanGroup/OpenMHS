## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation halts: the driver latches the e-stop and refuses further step and reset calls until mhs.safety.reset clears it; the joint state stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
