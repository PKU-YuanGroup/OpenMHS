## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: the runtime latches the e-stop before any further step or reset, and the environment state stays readable until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
