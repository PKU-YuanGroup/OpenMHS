## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The acknowledged /gazebo/pause_physics service pauses the Gazebo world: it stops stepping, so the clock and joint-state publications stop and the controllers cannot move the arm until something unpauses it (the resume command, the Gazebo GUI that the shipped launch opens, or another client). This is not a stopped-state measurement; a pending trajectory goal continues after an unpause. (achieved by `x-cap.zzy5510.pause.v1`).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
