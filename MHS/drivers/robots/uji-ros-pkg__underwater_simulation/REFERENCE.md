## Actions

### `publish_vehicle_twist`

`x-cap.uji-ros-pkg.vehicle_twist.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

Publish one geometry_msgs/TwistStamped velocity reference to /g500/twist. The receipt confirms only that rosbridge accepted the publication, not motion, arrival or stopping.

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No acknowledged stop: uwsim has no stop, pause or reset service or action, and the optional dynamics node's /dynamics/reset restores pose and velocity but keeps the thruster setpoint. Each /g500/twist message moves the vehicle once (twist times the wall time since the previous message), so after the latch no MHS message moves it, but nothing acknowledges that, and other publishers on /g500/twist, /g500/pose and the arm command topic are not fenced. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_vehicle_twist.angular_x`, `publish_vehicle_twist.angular_y`, `publish_vehicle_twist.angular_z`, `publish_vehicle_twist.linear_x`, `publish_vehicle_twist.linear_y`, `publish_vehicle_twist.linear_z`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
