## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: Simulated physics is paused through the acknowledged /gazebo/pause_physics service, so the Gazebo world stops stepping until any client calls /gazebo/unpause_physics (an openai_ros environment step does); the referee node, the planners and their retained commands are not stopped. (achieved by `x-cap.yizhengzhang1.pause_physics.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `command_base_velocity.angular_z`, `command_base_velocity.linear_x`, `command_base_velocity.linear_y`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
