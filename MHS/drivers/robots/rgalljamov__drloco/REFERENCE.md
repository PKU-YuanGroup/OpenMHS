## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: no step or reset is accepted after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the integration state and stored ctrl are kept and stay readable. Safety reset advances no ticks and does not clear ctrl; a later explicit step without an action reuses it (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
