## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true back: no world update runs, so no controller is updated and no joint command is written; it is the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator stops the trajectory (/robot_control_stop) and the custom controller and unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `command_joint_positions.joint_positions`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
