## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The simulation stops advancing: after an e-stop no cap.world.step.v1 or cap.world.reset.v1 call is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, while the integration state and stored ctrl remain readable. Safety reset advances no ticks and does not clear ctrl; a later explicit step without an action reuses it. This stops only this in-process model, not FlashCHORD or an external Isaac application. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
