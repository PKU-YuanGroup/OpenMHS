## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/fss/clock_control`.
- Safe state: The acknowledged fss/clock_control service is called with running=false and max_real_time_factor=0.0; in a root coordinator (no fss_time_parent_coordinator_endpoint, the launch default) should_advance then refuses to advance the shared simulation time until any client of fss/clock_control sends running=true, such as the coordinator UI's play button. A child coordinator keeps advancing its clock on every parent grant, so there the clock is not held. (achieved by `x-cap.shupx.pause.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `resume.max_real_time_factor`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
