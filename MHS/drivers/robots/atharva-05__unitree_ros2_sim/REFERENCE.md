## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/pause_physics`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused: two get_model_list answers 250 ms apart carried the same sim time, so no world update runs and gazebo_ros2_control writes no leg command; it is a frozen sim time, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator puts junior_ctrl in fixed_stand (key 2) or stops it, then unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
