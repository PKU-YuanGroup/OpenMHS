## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: software latch only: after an e-stop no further step or reset is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, an in-flight native step may finish and the last observation stays readable. Upstream UR5TaskSpaceEnv never pauses Gazebo (it always uses the no-op RobotConnection), so a live simulation keeps running and the trajectory controller holds the last commanded target. The latch sends no zero trajectory and does not stop a UR arm on scaled_pos_joint_traj_controller/command (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
