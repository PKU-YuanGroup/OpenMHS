## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulated world stops advancing: after an e-stop the runtime refuses step and reset until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; the environment has no native stop method and the readable state is kept (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
