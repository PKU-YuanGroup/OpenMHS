## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No acknowledged safe state is available: the node creates no arm, disarm or stop service and its only command inputs are the publish-only rc/override topics; a software latch cannot confirm that the flight controller stopped. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_rc_override.channels`, `publish_rc_override_raw.channels`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
