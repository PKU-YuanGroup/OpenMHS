## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/franka_gripper/stop`.
- Safe state: The /franka_gripper/stop action buffers a hold of the measured opening (half the sum of the absolute finger positions, zero speed target) and acknowledges success before the controller's next command update applies it; the finger PIDs then hold that opening while Gazebo and the arm keep running, a move or grasp goal still running ignores the stop for up to 5 s of ROS time (leaving the e-stop unconfirmed, latch held), and a later move or grasp goal from any ROS client replaces the hold. (achieved by `x-cap.justagist.stop.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_gripper.speed`, `move_gripper.width`, `grasp_gripper.speed`, `grasp_gripper.width`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
