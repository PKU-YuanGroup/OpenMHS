## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: No acknowledged stop exists in the pinned clone: both control endpoints are fire-and-forget std_msgs/Float64 publications, and the clone declares no pause, stop, controller-switch, reset or safe-state service or action. A zero-position publication is transmission only. The runtime software latch refuses new commands but cannot interrupt the external controllers or Gazebo, so a stock e-stop reports MHS.SAFETY.SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED and this package does not claim a stopped state. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_joint1_position.position`, `set_joint2_position.position`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
