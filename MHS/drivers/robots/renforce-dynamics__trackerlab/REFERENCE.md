## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the declared analytic fake stops advancing and retains its cached observation, target, tick and episode; no step or world reset is accepted while latched. mhs.safety.reset releases only the latch and advances no ticks. No pause or stop of an external Isaac Sim application is provided; a live adapter is outside this package (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `step.action.values`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
