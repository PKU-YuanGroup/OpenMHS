## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the software e-stop latch refuses further step and reset calls; an in-flight native control transition may finish, and the last observation, tick and episode counters stay readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `advance.action.values`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
