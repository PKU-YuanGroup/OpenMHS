## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `navigation.move_wheels`.
- Safe state: Both wheel targets are commanded to zero through Navigation.move_wheels (PyBullet VELOCITY_CONTROL, force 100) and the driver stops calling ADAM.step: in real-time mode (use_realtime, the ADAM default) the world keeps advancing and the wheel motors brake toward zero, in stepped mode it stays frozen until the next ADAM.step applies the zero targets, and with no wheel-velocity getter this is a commanded stop, not measured quiescence; the arms are not commanded. (achieved by `x-cap.mobile-robots-group-uc3m.zero_wheels.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `drive.angular_velocity`, `drive.duration_s`, `drive.linear_velocity`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
