## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: cap.world.step.v1 and cap.world.reset.v1 are refused after an e-stop until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the recorded world state stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
