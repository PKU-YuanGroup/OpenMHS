## Actions

### `move_usv_goal`

`x-cap.disaster-robotics-proalertas.move_usv_goal.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

Publish one nav_msgs/Odometry goal to the heading controller's move_usv/goal subscription; the controller publishes the two thruster commands without an acknowledgement. The receipt proves only that rosbridge accepted the publication, not that the boat moved or reached the goal.

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: Gazebo physics is paused through the acknowledged /gazebo/pause_physics service, so the simulated world stops stepping until /gazebo/unpause_physics is called; the service does not terminate the heading controller or clear its retained goal. (achieved by `x-cap.disaster-robotics-proalertas.pause_physics.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_usv_goal.x_m`, `move_usv_goal.y_m`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
