## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `ctrl_dual_arm`.
- Safe state: no safe state is confirmed: the e-stop requests the last buffered arm positions with zero feedforward torque, then fails SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED with the latch held; the undated buffer may be stale, the daemon continues publishing and may still have a previous target in flight, and non-arm motor commands remain enabled (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `move_joints.joint_positions`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
