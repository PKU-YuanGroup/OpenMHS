## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true back; the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: the box velocity stays stored and jackal_control is outside the clone. The Gazebo GUI or another client can unpause or step the world (a step advances it). A running respawn or unblock callback still completes its world edits. After mhs.safety.reset the world stays paused; delete planar_box, stop the Jackal nodes and unpause outside MHS (world.launch:9). (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
