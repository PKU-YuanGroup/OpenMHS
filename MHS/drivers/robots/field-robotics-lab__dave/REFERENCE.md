## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/hydrodynamics/set_current_velocity`.
- Safe state: The fixed set_current_velocity(0, 0, 0) request zeroes the global constant-current means and SetMean resets each process to its mean, so the global current is zero from the next world update while the velocity noiseAmp is 0; it is refused when a configured range excludes zero (the velocity mean may then already be zero). Transient-current vehicles keep their stratified and tidal current, Gazebo keeps running and any ROS client can set a new current. (achieved by `x-cap.field-robotics-lab.zero_current.v1`).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `set_current.horizontal_angle`, `set_current.velocity`, `set_current.vertical_angle`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
