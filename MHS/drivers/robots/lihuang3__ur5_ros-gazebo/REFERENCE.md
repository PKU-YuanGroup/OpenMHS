## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/gazebo/pause_physics`.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused and get_physics_properties read pause true back: no world update runs, so the arm controller, the vacuum plugins and the physics are frozen and each vacuum channel keeps its state; it is the paused flag, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator stops ur5_mp and its MoveIt goal and unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
