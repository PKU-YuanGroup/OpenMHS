## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulated spot world stops advancing: after an e-stop every step and reset is refused and the last observation stays readable until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch; there is no hardware to stop (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
