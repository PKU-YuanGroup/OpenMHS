### `publish_velocity_command`

`x-cap.evronix.velocity_command.v1` on `main`: invoke, risk R2, extension.

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/pause_physics`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: The Gazebo world is paused: two get_model_list answers 250 ms apart carried the same sim time, so no world update runs and gazebo_ros2_control applies no joint effort; it is a frozen sim time, not a measured standstill. No unpause is offered: an operator stops the policy node, then unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `publish_velocity_command.forward_velocity`, `publish_velocity_command.lateral_velocity`, `publish_velocity_command.yaw_rate`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
