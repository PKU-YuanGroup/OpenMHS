## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: The bridge stops advancing the model: after an e-stop it refuses step and reset, and the last integration state stays readable until an operator clears the latch. (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
