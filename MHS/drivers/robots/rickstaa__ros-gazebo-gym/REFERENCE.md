## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `gazebo.pause_proxy`.
- Safe state: the Gazebo world paused by /gazebo/pause_physics in the isolated host and confirmed by get_physics_properties pause True; the endpoint latch and the host fence refuse every step and reset; the controllers keep their last effort command, which acts again when a later step unpauses the world; another ROS client or the Gazebo GUI can unpause it. A host that is dead, unresponsive or not confirming is SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
