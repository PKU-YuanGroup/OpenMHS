## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, no native stop (software latch only).
- Safe state: the simulation stops advancing: after an e-stop no step or reset is accepted until mhs.safety.reset clears the latch, and the last observation stays readable (achieved by nothing declared).
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.

## Facts

- MuJoCoYAMEnv is a Gymnasium environment (gym.Env) for a bimanual YAM robot whose actions and observations use the 14D policy-space order [left_j1..6, left_gripper, right_j1..6, right_gripper] (upstream_code: abc_sim/env.py:72-77)
