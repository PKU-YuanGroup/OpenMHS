### `publish_velocity`

## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `/world/simple_tunnel/control`, latency 1500 ms.
- Safe state: The world simple_tunnel is paused: after the WorldControl pause two fresh stats samples, 40 ms or more apart within 600 ms, reported paused true, so the world does not step; it is the paused flag in that window, not a measured standstill, it needs the operator-run ros_gz bridges, and no unpause is offered because CHAMP keeps req_vel_ and the JointTrajectoryController keeps its targets, so the operator zeros /cmd_vel and unpauses outside MHS. (achieved by nothing declared).
