## Safety

- E-stop: software, latching, native stop `send_rc_control`.
- Safe state: unconfirmed: a zero RC publish is attempted, but the clone supplies no acknowledgement or stopped-state read-back and suppresses send errors; the latch refuses new commands, while the external simulator may continue moving; e-stop reports SAFE_STATE_UNREACHED and recovery requires a reviewed stop/confirmation path outside this package (achieved by nothing declared).
- Operator-required limits not yet set: `speed`, `rotation_speed`, `takeoff.takeoff_height`, `takeoff.takeoff_speed`, `land.landing_speed`, `move.distance`, `rotate.angle`, `rotate_camera.angle`. Hardware use is refused (MHS.SAFETY.LIMITS_UNSET) until site-limits.json supplies them.
- OpenMHS is not a safety certification and not a safety-rated channel; the physical e-stop stays authoritative.
