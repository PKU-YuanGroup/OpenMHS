## Safety

The risk floor of a device is the highest catalogue `risk_class` of its classes (CAP-017); a driver may declare a higher risk, never a lower one. Hardware at R1 or above needs an `@estop` and a non-empty safe state. A real OT-2 or Flex pipettes and moves labware; tip tracking and contamination are the driver's job.
