## Launch

Every driver package starts the same way; per-repo configuration (environment id, model path, port, baud rate, VISA resource) lives in the package's `driver.py`, never in launch flags:

```
mhs serve <driver-dir>
```

A package is done when `python3 -m mhs.driver check <driver-dir> --backend fake` exits 0 (evidence `contract_checked`; a fake never yields an MHS-Device level).

## Applicability

A loadable MJCF scene or robot model with actuators, with a mujoco, dm_control or mujoco_py import, or in a pure model repository: triage rule 9, `family:mujoco`. The card's `models` names the model to load (the first entry is the main scene or robot model); object and fixture assets, MJCF fragments and copies under examples or tests never qualify. Environments that compose their MJCF at run time (robosuite, LIBERO) have no main model in the clone and are recon cards. It copies the structure of the Gymnasium exemplar.

## Safety

The risk floor of a device is the highest catalogue `risk_class` of its classes (CAP-017); a driver may declare a higher risk, never a lower one. Hardware at R1 or above needs an `@estop` and a non-empty safe state. A model of a real robot gives the hardware no lower risk class; the hardware device takes its floor from the catalogue.

## Fidelity

The time step is declared in the MJCF; mesh and texture assets carry their own licences.

## Pitfalls

qpos-only snapshots miss controller, RNG and time state; mj_step counts are not wall-clock; rendering needs a GL context.
