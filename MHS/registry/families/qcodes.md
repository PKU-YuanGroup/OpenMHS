## Applicability

QCoDeS instrument classes: triage rule 9, `family:qcodes`. `QcodesDriver` is a configuration-only helper over the Python-object bridge. Each field is a `Field` in `parameters` plus one `parameter_bindings` entry: `parameter` (the attribute path of a Parameter), `get: True`, `set` (equal to `Field.writable`), `timeout_ms` (1..60000) and `source_ref` (the `path:LINE[-END]` of its constructor). Gate G7 proves a `self.<name> = Parameter(...)` assignment in `__init__` of the card's entry class or its in-clone ancestry, with `Parameter` imported from `qcodes.parameters` and an explicit `get_cmd` (and `set_cmd` for a write); a same-file factory needs `factory_ref`. Parameters created by `add_parameter`, channel lists, submodules or dynamic factories are not provable today, and most QCoDeS drivers use `add_parameter`: such a card stops `blocked` with the G7 finding `QCODES_PARAMETER_SOURCE`.

The [PM100D exemplar](../../drivers/labs/qcodes__qcodes_contrib_drivers/driver.py) serves a Thorlabs PM100D optical power meter (R1): seven Parameters, two writes through `cap.device.configure.v1` (one `Limit.operator_required`), the stop `write("ABOR")` (the abort command the source sends before it re-initializes a measurement), and a source-cited reason for every Parameter it does not serve. The [QCoDeS driver guide](../../sdk/docs/qcodes-driver.md) gives the complete contract.

## Capability mapping

| native call | OpenMHS target | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Parameter.get()` | a `parameters` Field and a `parameter_bindings` entry with `get: True` | every read is a new native get; `cache` and `get_latest` are never substituted |
| `Parameter.set(value)` | a writable Field (`set: True`), served as `cap.device.configure.v1` | the whole setter is awaited, steps and delays included; a QCoDeS validator becomes a sourced `Limit` only where it bounds the device, else `Limit.operator_required` |
| Parameter `unit`, `scale`, `get_parser`, `val_mapping` | the Field's type and unit follow what `get()` returns after the parsers | a unit OpenMHS does not know (dB) goes into the description, not `unit`; a `scale` without a `get_parser` makes the upstream get fail on the reply string |
| the instrument's `write()` / `ask()` | a native stop: `safety_bindings.estop` with `invoke: write` and a stop or abort command the source itself sends | the stop shares the VISA session with a running call; prefer a write that expects no reply |
| `Instrument.close()` | `native_close = "close"` | teardown, never a capability |
| construction with a VISA address | lifecycle: the `native.py` factory | QCoDeS constructors talk to the instrument before they return; disclose those commands |
| `add_parameter`, channels, submodules, `ArrayParameter` | not expressible | G7 reports `QCODES_PARAMETER_SOURCE` or `QCODES_UNDECLARED_MEMBER`: stop `blocked` |

Class ids come from `python3 -m mhs.driver catalog search <words>`. The deprecated classes cap.device.describe.v1, cap.world.state.v1, cap.world.snapshot.v1 and cap.world.restore.v1 are replaced by the protocol methods mhs.devices.get, mhs.resource.read, mhs.snapshot and mhs.restore.

## Safety

The risk floor of a device is the highest catalogue `risk_class` of its classes (CAP-017); a driver may declare a higher risk, never a lower one. Hardware at R1 or above needs a native stop (`safety_bindings.estop`) and a non-empty safe state. Sources, heaters, magnets and RF outputs: parameter validators are not safety limits, risk follows the hazard table of the safety specification, and a model-authored R2 or R3 package never runs its hardware backend before a human signs `reviewed_by`. Cancelling a Parameter set is not a stop acknowledgement.

## Fidelity

A source-shaped fake holds one stand-in per bound Parameter (`get()`, and `set()` where the upstream has a `set_cmd`) plus the instrument methods the driver calls, with placeholder values typed as the upstream parsers return them. It yields `contract_checked` evidence only: VISA timing, SCPI error queues and instrument state machines are not modelled.

## Pitfalls

Contributed drivers vary in quality: check every parser, scale and `set_cmd` placeholder against the source. A `MEAS:...?` read reconfigures the instrument; a constructor that writes settings belongs to the hardware factory, not to a capability.
