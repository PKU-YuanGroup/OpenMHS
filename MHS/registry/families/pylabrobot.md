## Applicability

An upstream whose control interface is a PyLabRobot `LiquidHandler` front end, used directly or held by an upstream object (a digital twin's `env.lh`). The package sets one `plr_profile = PlrProfile(...)` and no methods; every capability is generated. Exemplar: oelemento/star-sim (simulated STARlet twin). Other PyLabRobot machines (plate readers, incubators, spectrophotometers, scanners) use a direct `Driver` or `PythonObjectDriver`; a class that only borrows PyLabRobot method names, a firmware protocol or a remote service without a PyLabRobot front end uses a direct `Driver` (`DEVIATION FROM THE CARD`). A card whose entry symbols do not include the class the package drives needs a maintainer override first (`G7.NOT_AN_ENTRY_SYMBOL`). Single-step aspirate/dispense and the cap.fluid.transfer.v1 / cap.fluid.mix.v1 / cap.fluid.level.v1 classes are not bound by this profile (see the helper guide).

## Safety

The risk floor of a device is the highest catalogue `risk_class` of its classes (CAP-017); a driver may declare a higher risk, never a lower one. Hardware at R1 or above needs an `@estop` and a non-empty safe state. Pipetting and gantry motion are R2 (MHS-004 hazard table); the composite operations carry R2 in every mode. A hardware backend without `plr_profile.stop` is refused before its factory runs and gate G3 refuses the package: stop with `blocked` rather than invent a stop or use `stop()`/`teardown`. A `PlrStop` description says what it does not stop (a firmware halt may let the running sequence complete) and must be safe to repeat: the server retries it in the background until it confirms. The bridge sends no PyLabRobot step after an e-stop, cancellation or session close; a step in progress continues. After a failure or an e-stop the tips stay mounted and v1 has no operation that clears them: an operator clears the head (a simulated twin restarts). The loader refuses an unreviewed model-written R2/R3 hardware backend.

## Fidelity

A package fake composes `mhs.testing.pylabrobot` (tip and volume bookkeeping only: no motion, timing, liquid classes or physics); its capacities and starting liquid are placeholders. Evidence stays contract_checked; a fake gate never establishes an MHS-Device level or PyLabRobot behaviour.

## Pitfalls

PyLabRobot tip tracking is off unless the upstream switches it on: fresh-tip selection needs the cited `set_tip_tracking(True)` line. Vendored PyLabRobot copies differ by version. Deck layouts often live in scripts or operator code; only labware a pinned line names can be declared. Upstream protocols often discard tips in a `finally` block; the helper deliberately does not. The gate reads every profile reference as code (`ast`, one line, operation spans of at most 40 lines). The gate's conformance suite repeats one payload, so give every operation an `alternate` fixture. A deck of more than about ten plate names exceeds the 2048-byte tool budget (G8.TOOL_SIZE).
