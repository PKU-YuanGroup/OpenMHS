## Launch

Every driver package starts the same way; per-repo configuration (environment id, model path, port, baud rate, VISA resource) lives in the package's `driver.py`, never in launch flags:

```
mhs serve <driver-dir>
```

A package is done when `python3 -m mhs.driver check <driver-dir> --backend fake` exits 0 (evidence `contract_checked`; a fake never yields an MHS-Device level).

## Safety

The risk floor of a device is the highest catalogue `risk_class` of its classes (CAP-017); a driver may declare a higher risk, never a lower one. Hardware at R1 or above needs an `@estop` and a non-empty safe state. An environment's motion and manipulation classes keep their catalogue risk classes for the agent contract even in simulation. `terminated` is not task success.

## Fidelity

Fidelity is defined by the engine behind the environment; declare action space, control frequency, horizon and seeding in the fidelity contract.

## Pitfalls

Wrappers clip or normalise actions; reset may not reseed every RNG; vectorised environments are not one device; rendering may need a display or GPU.
